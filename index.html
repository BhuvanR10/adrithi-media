LEGAL ARCHIVES & CLIENT PRIVACY

Terms & Conditions of Service

LAST REVISED: NOVEMBER 2026 • ADRITHI MEDIA

1. Commissions & Reservation

All photographic and cinematic assignments undertaken by Adrithi Media ("The Studio") require a formal booking confirmation and an agreed retainer deposit. Dates are reserved on a dedicated basis and cannot be guaranteed without receipt of the deposit agreement.

2. Artistic Style & Creative Discretion

Clients acknowledge that Adrithi Media operates with an intentional fine-art, documentary, and cinematic editorial aesthetic. Lighting conditions, atmospheric tones, color palette grading, and artistic composition remain at the professional discretion of the principal artists.

3. Image Archiving & Delivery Timelines

Curated highlight previews are typically made available within 7 to 14 days of an assignment. Complete master high-resolution archives undergo rigorous editorial post-production and are delivered via private cloud gallery within 4 to 8 weeks. Clients are advised to maintain redundant local copies of their delivered archives.

4. Copyright & Reproduction Rights

Under statutory copyright law, all original negative files, raw media, and edited photographs produced by Adrithi Media remain the sole intellectual property of Adrithi Media. Clients receive a perpetual, non-exclusive license for personal, non-commercial reproduction, private printing, and digital sharing across personal networks.

5. Rescheduling & Unforeseen Circumstances

In the event of event rescheduling due to weather, family emergencies, or force majeure events, The Studio will make every professional effort to accommodate alternate dates subject to existing calendar commitments.

Privacy & Data Governance Policy

LAST REVISED: NOVEMBER 2026 • ADRITHI MEDIA

1. Collection of Client Information

Adrithi Media collects necessary contact details (such as your name, telephone number, email address, event dates, and venue locations) solely to facilitate consultations, contract execution, and project coordination.

2. Archival Imagery & Portfolio Showcases

We respect client discretion and privacy above all. Portfolio displays on our website, social media chronicles, and archival previews celebrate authentic artistic work and are published with client awareness and consent. If you require private or embargoed coverage for high-profile weddings or ceremonies, please notify our studio team during booking.

3. Data Protection & Zero Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose client personal information or unreleased photographs to third-party advertisers or unauthorized external brokers. Data is handled strictly within secure SSL-encrypted cloud storage and internal production workflows.

4. Your Access & Rectification Rights

Clients may at any time request an inventory of their personal details stored with us, request updates to their contact records, or inquire about their archival storage by reaching out via our direct contact channels.

Cookie Permission & Web Technologies

LAST REVISED: NOVEMBER 2026 • ADRITHI MEDIA

1. Why We Use Cookies

Cookies and local browser storage are utilized on this site solely to enhance your viewing experience, preserve responsive layout preferences, ensure smooth full-screen image transitions, and optimize high-resolution gallery caching without requiring re-downloads.

2. Types of Cookies Used

  • Essential Session Data: Necessary for navigation, secure modal transitions, and responsive screen scaling.
  • Performance & Fluid Cache: Minimizes server latency by remembering recently viewed photography sleeves and thumbnails.
  • Consent Token: Remembers your decision regarding cookie permissions so you are not prompted repeatedly.

3. Manage Your Preferences

You can adjust or revoke your cookie consent at any time. When you choose to accept, all performance optimizations are enabled. If you decline, only non-tracking essential session variables will operate.
