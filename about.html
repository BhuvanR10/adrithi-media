ABOUT THE STUDIO

Preserving Stories with Honesty, Reverence, and Art.

Adrithi Media is an independent photography and cinematography studio based in Bengaluru. We document sacred weddings, intimate ceremonies, and generational legacies across India and worldwide.

We chase the felt over the rehearsed, the sacred over the theatrical.

True celebration is never about manufactured drama or rigid, choreographed posing. It lives in the fleeting, unscripted truths: the quiet exchange between partners before entering the mandap, a tear held back during Kanyadaan, the celebratory burst of yellow petals in Haldi laughter, and the sacred reverence of vows spoken around the holy fire.

Founded in Bengaluru, our studio brings together cinema-grade optical sensors and meticulous in-house color grading. Every frame is treated as an irreplaceable family historical record, curated with patience and deep cultural reverence.
